# Structural Design Patterns — Map

```text
STRUCTURAL PATTERNS

Adapter
→ HOW do I make incompatible interfaces work together?

Decorator
→ HOW do I add behavior without changing the original class?

Facade
→ HOW do I provide a simple interface over a complex subsystem?

Proxy
→ HOW do I control access to another object?

Composite
→ HOW do I treat individual objects and groups uniformly?

Bridge
→ HOW do I separate two dimensions that should vary independently?
```
